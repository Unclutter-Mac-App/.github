## What is Unclutter?  

**Unclutter** is a beautifully crafted macOS utility designed to streamline your daily workflow. It serves as a **central hub** for your clipboard, notes, and files — letting you drag, drop, and store information instantly without cluttering your desktop.  

With a simple gesture — just swipe down from the top of the screen — you gain access to three smart panels:  

- **Files Panel:** Quickly store temporary files or frequently used documents. Think of it as a mini desktop that doesn’t get messy.  
- **Notes Panel:** Create and manage text snippets, to-do lists, or quick thoughts without launching a separate app.  
- **Clipboard History:** Keep track of everything you copy and instantly retrieve past items, even after reboots.  

Unclutter isn’t just convenient — it’s deeply integrated into macOS, supporting drag-and-drop actions, automatic syncing, and customizable themes. Whether you’re coding, designing, or studying, Unclutter makes multitasking faster and more intuitive.  

Optimized for **Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3)**, Unclutter runs efficiently without draining battery life. It’s the perfect tool for anyone who values speed, minimalism, and organization on their Mac.  

If you love keeping your digital space clean, **Unclutter** will feel like an invisible assistant always ready to help.
